<p align="center">Reliu is an iPhone game about building everyday health habits. You start with a stone companion and a picture of your own routine: how you sleep, move, eat, and handle stress. Wearable data and lab results can help shape your plan.</p>

<p align="center">Each morning, Reliu picks one thing worth noticing and gives you one to three quests for the day. If you slept less than usual, for example, your quests get lighter without changing your longer-term goal.</p>

<p align="center">Finish your quests and your companion moves another step along the trail. Over time, your stone grows into jade. You can also play with friends and build streaks together.</p>
